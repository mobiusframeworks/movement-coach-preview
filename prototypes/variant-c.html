About

I'm Alex Wright — hand balancing, coordination, and movement patterns coach. I've spent years developing my own practice and now help others build real physical capability at any level.

My approach is simple: clear progressions, honest feedback, and a practice you can sustain for life.

What I teach

Hand Balancing

From your first kick-up to freestanding handstands and beyond. Progressive, skill-based coaching.

Coordination

Body awareness, timing, and control. Build the foundation that makes everything else easier.

Movement Patterns

Flowing, practical movement. Connect individual skills into usable, real-world capability.

One-on-One Coaching

Personalized sessions tailored to your level, goals, and how you learn best.
