About

I'm Alex Wright, the coach behind The Wright Practices. My own practice moves across hand balancing, strength training, surfing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, boxing, mobility, and somatic movement.

I coach because I believe physical training should help you understand your body—not just complete exercises. My approach combines strength, mobility, coordination, skill development, and embodied movement so you can build useful capability through clear progressions, honest feedback, and a practice you can sustain for life.

The approach

More than a list of exercises

My approach combines strength training, mobility, coordination, skill development, and embodied movement. Rather than simply prescribing exercises, I help you understand how to move, develop awareness, and build skills progressively.

  • 1

    Assessment

    We start with where you are — your current capabilities, goals, and any limitations.

  • 2

    Foundations

    Build the base: joint preparation, body awareness, and fundamental positions.

  • 3

    Progressions

    Step-by-step skill development. Clear milestones so you always know what to work on.

  • 4

    Practice plan

    You leave with a simple, repeatable practice you can do on your own.
