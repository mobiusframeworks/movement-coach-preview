About

I'm Alex Wright, the coach behind The Wright Practices — offering one-on-one personal training and movement coaching in Santa Cruz, with an emphasis on hand balancing, coordination, and movement patterns.

My approach is simple: clear progressions, honest feedback, and a practice you can sustain for life.

What I teach

Hand Balancing

From your first kick-up to freestanding handstands and beyond. Progressive, skill-based coaching.

Coordination

Body awareness, timing, and control. Build the foundation that makes everything else easier.

Movement Patterns

Flowing, practical movement. Connect individual skills into usable, real-world capability.

One-on-One Coaching

Personalized sessions tailored to your level, goals, and how you learn best.

What working together looks like

  • 1

    Assessment

    We start with where you are — your current capabilities, goals, and any limitations.

  • 2

    Foundations

    Build the base: joint preparation, body awareness, and fundamental positions.

  • 3

    Progressions

    Step-by-step skill development. Clear milestones so you always know what to work on.

  • 4

    Practice plan

    You leave with a simple, repeatable practice you can do on your own.
